Product Safety Information

Material Disclosure (California AB 1200)

Our stainless steel cookware and utensils are made from food-grade 18/8 (304) stainless steel, which contains:

  • Iron (Fe)
  • Chromium (Cr) CAS 7440-47-3
  • Nickel (Ni) CAS 7440-02-0
  • Manganese (Mn) CAS 7439-96-5
  • Molybdenum (Mo) CAS 7439-98-7

These materials are widely used in food-contact products and meet FDA standards for food safety. Under normal use, these materials do not pose a health risk.

Natural Wood Care

Our Rosewood Handle Collection features natural wood handles. Wood is a natural material with unique grain patterns. To maintain the beauty and longevity of your wooden handle:

  • Hand wash recommended to preserve the wooden handle
  • The stainless steel head is dishwasher safe; hand wash wooden handle
  • Dry thoroughly after each use
  • Do not soak the wooden handle in water for extended periods
  • Occasionally treat with food-safe mineral oil to maintain finish
