Under normal use, these materials do not pose a health risk.

Natural Wood Handle Care

Select products in our collection feature natural wood handles. Wood is a natural material with unique grain patterns that adds warmth and beauty to your kitchen.

Care Instructions:

  • Hand wash with warm, soapy water
  • Not dishwasher safe — high heat may damage the wooden handle
  • Dry thoroughly after each use
  • Do not soak in water for extended periods
  • Occasionally treat with food-safe mineral oil to maintain finish

Note: Each wooden handle is individually grained and may vary slightly in color and pattern — this is a natural characteristic of wood.
