Our stainless steel cookware and utensils are made from food-grade 18/8 (304) stainless steel. These materials are widely used in food-contact products and meet FDA standards for food safety.

Material Components (with CAS Numbers)

  • Iron (Fe)
  • Chromium (Cr) CAS 7440-47-3
  • Nickel (Ni) CAS 7440-02-0
  • Manganese (Mn) CAS 7439-96-5
  • Molybdenum (Mo) CAS 7439-98-7

Under normal use, these materials do not pose a health risk.

Natural Wood Handle Care

Select products feature natural wood handles. Wood adds warmth and beauty to your kitchen, and each piece has unique grain patterns.

Care Instructions

  • Hand wash with warm, soapy water
  • Not dishwasher safe — high heat may damage wood
  • Dry thoroughly after each use
  • Do not soak in water
  • Treat occasionally with food-safe mineral oil
Note: Each wooden handle is individually grained and may vary in color and pattern — this is natural.
